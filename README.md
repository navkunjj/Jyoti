# JYOTI — A Spark of Diwali
### A Cinematic, Interactive Indian Diwali Experience

**JYOTI** is an immersive digital homage to Diwali — the Indian Festival of Lights. Built with React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, and Lenis, it evokes the serenity, architectural majesty, and artistic traditions of Diwali.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Color Palette

Rooted in the twilight riverside aesthetic of the palace ghats:
- **Midnight Teal (`#102C38`)**: Twilight skies and deep river waters.
- **Deep Navy (`#101B35`)**: Architectural shadows and night celestial depth.
- **Muted Lavender (`#A99BBF`)**: Atmospheric horizon haze and decorative accents.
- **Lotus Pink (`#E98EAF`)**: Hanging bougainvillea petals and lotus motifs.
- **Soft Blush (`#F4C4D2`)**: Secondary floral highlights and delicate accents.
- **Warm Ivory (`#F3EAE2`)**: Hand-carved marble pillars, editorial typography, and text.
- **Diya Amber (`#FDB863` / `#FFE29A`)**: Authentic, warm terracotta and brass lamp flames.

## 🏛️ Interactive Chapters

1. **Chapter 01 — Welcome (Hero)**
   - 100svh cinematic full-screen edge-to-edge landscape.
   - Traditional Indian carved stone pillar, cascading bougainvillea, swaying brass lantern, crescent moon, and riverside palace ghats.
   - Live bougainvillea petal drift with mouse breeze response.
   - Interactive courtyard diya with sound chime and ember sparkles.
   - Staggered GSAP entrance typography.

2. **Chapter 02 — The Art of Diwali**
   - Editorial exploration of sacred Indian artistic traditions:
     - **Rangoli & Alpana**: Sacred threshold mandalas.
     - **Madhubani Art**: Ancient Mithila mythological narratives.
     - **Mitti Ke Diye**: Terracotta oil lamps spun on manual wheels.
     - **Phoolon Ki Sajawat**: Marigold, bougainvillea & mango leaf torans.
     - **Sacred Kolams**: Geometric dot matrices drawn at dawn.
   - Interactive modal lightbox with cultural lore, historical context, and spiritual significance.

3. **Chapter 03 — Diwali Across India**
   - Pinned horizontal scrolling experience powered by **GSAP ScrollTrigger**:
     - **Ayodhya**: The Grand Deepotsav & Lord Rama's triumphant return.
     - **Varanasi**: Dev Deepawali across the 84 holy ghats of the Ganges.
     - **West Bengal**: Midnight Shyama Puja & devotion to Maa Kali.
     - **South India**: Naraka Chaturdashi & pre-dawn Ganga Snanam.

4. **Chapter 04 — The Celebration**
   - The emotional human tapestry of Diwali:
     - **Deepdan**: Kindling lights from heart to heart.
     - **Mithai & Pakwaan**: Saffron and ghee sweets shared with neighbors.
     - **Bandhanwar & Sajavat**: Sanctifying homes with brass urns and marigolds.
     - **Milan & Parivar**: Generations reuniting across distances.

5. **Chapter 05 — Send Wishes & Interactive Diya**
   - Sacred Upanishadic shloka: *“Om Asato Ma Sadgamaya, Tamaso Ma Jyotirgamaya…”*
   - Interactive Diya station with live lamp kindling counter.
   - Personalized Diwali Greeting Card generator with 4 blessing archetypes (*Jyoti*, *Shubh Labh*, *Shanti*, *Anand*).
   - Sky Lantern release simulation that floats glowing lanterns into the starry night with confetti bursts.
   - Copy-to-clipboard greeting for sharing.

6. **Navigation & Controls**
   - Floating header with dynamic glassmorphism and smooth section navigation.
   - Fixed left vertical chapter progress bar with animated active indicators.
   - **Meditative Ambient Soundscape**: Web Audio API ambient tanpura drone, river water murmur, and meditative temple chimes with mute/unmute control.
